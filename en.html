OUR PROMISE

Our Result Guarantees

Adult 1-Day S-Turn Guarantee

Complete the full 6-hour 1-day course and still can't link S-turns on a beginner run — we give you 3 extra hours, free of charge.

The 3 free hours can go on any day of your trip (we won't make you ski 9 hours in one day). If your trip has no room, keep them for next season. Booked 2+ days? We judge by the end of your first 6-hour day, and your day-2 lesson goes ahead as planned.

Conditions: lessons at Hoshino TOMAMU or Asahikawa; 1:1 or 1:2 only (not 1:3 or 1:4), complete beginner or only 1–2 prior sessions, in good health, full 6 hours completed, resort open and operating normally.

Kids Hand-to-Solo Guarantee

Your child completes the 6-hour 1:1 course but still can't ski independently and stop on their own — we add 3 extra hours, free of charge.

The 3 free hours can go on any day of your trip (we won't make you ski 9 hours in one day). If your trip has no room, keep them for next season.

Conditions: lessons at Hoshino TOMAMU or Asahikawa; child aged 10 or older, 1:1, full 6 hours completed. (Kids as young as 3 can enroll, but the guarantee applies from age 10 — please note this before booking.)

PEACE OF MIND

Why parents can trust us with their children

What parents really want to know isn't "are they patient?" — it's "who's accountable if something goes wrong?" Here's exactly how we handle it.

Hoshino TOMAMU Official School. Snowi is operated by Shinemi GK and is an officially recognised ski school at Hoshino TOMAMU resort. Our credentials are verifiable — we're not a random instructor on the street.

Emi goes to hospital with you and handles it in Japanese. If an accident requires medical care, Emi goes with you in person and speaks directly with the doctors, nurses and insurer in fluent Japanese. No translation apps, no waiting for an interpreter, no mistranslations slowing down your care. You're not on your own.

End-of-lesson progress report. After every lesson the instructor gives a verbal summary of which stage of our method your child reached. Progress you can see.

What we do if something goes wrong
  1. Assess immediately on the spot; call ski patrol if needed
  2. Contact you right away with a clear account of what happened and what comes next
  3. Assist with insurance claims and medical coordination
  4. Emi goes to hospital with you and deals with the hospital and insurer directly in Japanese: no translation apps, no waiting, no mistranslations
  5. Lesson fees handled on a case-by-case basis; full terms in Disclaimer below

Snow & Safety Statement

Skiing and snowboarding carry inherent risks. Accidents may result from snow conditions, weather, terrain, or personal physical condition. We reserve the right to modify, postpone, or cancel any lesson on the day based on actual on-site conditions, visibility, and avalanche risk assessments, with participant safety as the absolute priority. All courses include up to ¥200 million accident insurance; actual coverage is subject to the insurer's policy terms. Participants must accurately disclose their health status, prior injury history, and experience level before the lesson. Minors must be enrolled with guardian consent or accompaniment.
