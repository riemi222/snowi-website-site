HOW WE TEACH

Hand-to-Solo — Our Kids' Ski Method

The biggest risk on day one isn't a fall — it's a child who gets scared and never wants to ski again. This is Snowi's own named progression: five stages that take a child from held by two hands to skiing out on their own.

01

Feel the Glide

Instructor holds the board with both hands and controls speed while coaching the child on how to stop

02

Ease the Grip

Gradually reducing support so the child begins using their own leg strength

03

One Hand

Child is managing most of the movement; instructor switches to single-hand assist

04

Fingertip Hold

Instructor holds only the child's hand; the child is skiing independently

05

Fully Solo

Child can ski and stop on their own — that's when we truly let go

06

Every Chairlift, Together

Instructor accompanies the child on every single ride — no exceptions, no child left alone

Exact timing depends on the child's confidence and muscle development. Most kids complete the five skiing stages within a 6-hour 1:1 day. Full chairlift escort is our safety promise to every child from start to finish. At the end of the lesson we tell you exactly which stage your child reached.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

I've never skied before and I'm not in my twenties — is that okay?
Absolutely. Most of our students are first-timers. We adjust pace to your energy — relaxed and confident beats fit and tense every time. If you want to try before committing, start with the 3-hour taster.
Can adults really learn S-turns in one day?
Most adult beginners can link turns on a beginner run within 6 hours. It depends on your body awareness and confidence. If you don't get there, our S-Turn Guarantee gives you 3 hours the next day at no extra charge (1:1 or 1:2 only).
Will the instructor speak English / Chinese?
Yes. The founder and most of our partner instructors can teach in Chinese or English. Tell us your preference in the booking form and we'll match you with the right instructor.
Which resort do lessons take place at?
The majority of lessons run at Hoshino TOMAMU. We also partner with 15 other Hokkaido resorts. If you have a specific resort in mind, let us know in the notes field.
What if conditions are bad or the resort is closed?
We reserve the right to adjust, postpone, or cancel lessons based on on-site snow conditions, visibility, and avalanche risk. Safety always comes before lesson continuity. See the Snow & Safety Statement below for details.
Do I need to bring my own gear?
No. We can arrange rental equipment for you. Just tell us in the notes field and we'll sort it out ahead of your lesson.
Can children join lessons?
Yes. The kids' day course is 1:1 only, open from age 3. Our outcome guarantee (free 3-hour add-on if the goal isn't reached) applies from age 10. Please note your child's age in the booking form.
How do I pay?
Introductory courses (3hr, 4hr, 6hr) accept secure online payment via Stripe after we confirm your dates and group size. Advanced courses are quoted individually — we contact you first, then send payment details.
