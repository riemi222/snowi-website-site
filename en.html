HOW WE TEACH

Hand-in-Hand to Letting Go — Our Children's Ski Introduction Method

The biggest fear on a child's first day on snow isn't falling — it's getting scared and never wanting to ski again. This is Snowi's own named teaching method, letting your child progress step by step from having their hand held to skiing off on their own, all on day one.

01

Feel the Glide

The instructor holds the board the whole time to control speed, while teaching your child to brake on command

02

Gradual Release

The instructor gradually eases off, letting your child start moving with their own leg strength

03

One-Hand Support

Your child can now control most movements themselves, so the instructor switches to one-handed support

04

Just a Hand to Hold

The instructor only holds your child's hand while they glide on their own

05

Letting Go Completely

Your child can glide and brake entirely on their own — this is what "letting go" really means

06

Always By Their Side on the Lift

The instructor guarantees to ride every chairlift with your child — never alone, always safe

Actual timing varies with each child's confidence and muscle development, but most children complete the first five gliding stages within one day (a 6-hour 1-on-1 lesson). Riding every lift together is our safety commitment to every child, start to finish. At the end of the lesson, we'll tell you which stage your child reached that day.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I join with zero ski experience?
Yes. Our instructors adjust the pace to your actual level, starting from putting on gear and riding the lift — no prior experience needed. We recommend starting with the 3- or 4-hour beginner course.
Do the instructors speak Chinese?
Yes. The founder and most of our instructors can teach in Chinese; you can note your preferred teaching language on the booking form and we'll match you with a suitable instructor.
Which resort are lessons held at?
Most of our lessons currently take place at Hoshino TOMAMU, where we're deeply familiar with the terrain and conditions. If you need a different resort, just let us know in the notes and we'll do our best to arrange it.
Will the lesson be cancelled in bad snow or weather?
We decide whether to adjust or postpone based on on-site conditions and a safety assessment — see "Snow Conditions & Safety Statement" below for details. Safety always comes before keeping to schedule.
Do I need to bring my own ski gear?
No — we can arrange equipment rental for you. Just let us know your needs in the notes field.
Can children join the lessons?
Yes — we have extensive experience teaching families and children, adjusting our approach to each child's age and physical ability. Please note your child's age in the booking form.
What payment methods are accepted?
Beginner courses support secure online credit card payment (via Stripe) — once your group size and date are confirmed, you can complete payment. Advanced courses involve more complex venue and instructor scheduling, so we confirm details first before payment.

Snow Conditions & Safety Statement

Skiing and snowboarding carry inherent risk, and accidents can occur due to snow conditions, weather, terrain, or an individual's physical condition. We reserve the right to adjust, postpone, or cancel a lesson based on on-site snow conditions, visibility, avalanche risk, and other safety factors, with participant safety always the top priority. Every course includes accident insurance up to ¥200 million; claim terms and coverage are subject to the insurer's actual policy conditions.

Participants must honestly disclose their health condition, any prior injuries, and their skiing experience, and assess for themselves whether a particular course level is suitable — the advanced course (Park／Powder-Tree Guide) in particular requires the ability to comfortably S-turn on red (blue) runs. Minors must be accompanied by, or have the consent of, a guardian to book.
