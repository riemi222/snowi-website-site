FAQ

Frequently asked questions

I've never skied and I'm not young. Can I still learn?
Yes. Most of our students are first-timers. We start with putting on gear and riding the lift, and adjust to your energy. To link turns in a day, take the One-Day S-Turn Course; to try it first, take the 3-hour taster.
Can I really link S-turns in one day?
Most adult beginners link turns on a beginner run within one 6-hour day; progress depends on fitness and confidence. The One-Day S-Turn Course at 1:1 or 1:2 comes with our guarantee: not there by day's end? We add 3 hours free the next day.
How old do children need to be?
Kids can join the Hand-Hold to Let-Go day from age 3. It's 1:1 only and the instructor rides every lift with your child. The guarantee (an extra 3 hours if they can't glide and stop on their own) applies from age 10.
Can adults and kids take lessons together?
Yes: book the Family Group Course: one instructor teaches two of you together (for example 1 adult + 1 child) at the 1:2 rate. Larger families are split into pairs, each with its own instructor.
How is the price calculated?
Prices are per group, based on the instructor-to-student ratio, hours and low/peak season, so the more people, the less each person pays. Groups of more than 4 are split with more instructors. Lesson fee only: lift tickets and gear are extra. Pick a course, group size and date in the booking form below to see the price right away.
How do I book, and when do I pay?
Send the booking form; nothing to pay yet. Once we confirm your instructor (usually within 48 hours), we email you a link to pay a 50% deposit, and you're booked. Pay the balance before your first lesson, any time from My bookings.
Can I change or cancel?
To change dates, message us on WhatsApp or LINE and we'll do our best. Cancellations: 31+ days before the lesson, 50% of the amount paid is refunded; 8–30 days, 30%; within 7 days or no-show, no refund. Stripe fees are deducted from refunds.
Do I need my own gear?
No. In the booking form, choose "Need rental gear arranged" for each student and add height, weight and shoe size, and we'll arrange it. Gear and lift tickets aren't included in the lesson fee.
Which resort are lessons at?
Mainly Hoshino TOMAMU, where our instructors know the terrain and snow well. If you'd like another resort, choose it in the form or tell us in the note and we'll try to arrange it.
What languages do instructors speak?
Our instructors teach in English. Lessons in Chinese, Japanese, or Chinese + English are also available.
Is insurance included? What if there's an accident?
Every lesson includes accident insurance up to ¥200 million (claims follow the insurer's terms). If anything happens, the instructor responds right away and contacts you, and Emi herself can go to the hospital with you to translate.
What if the weather is bad?
We adjust or postpone based on snow conditions and safety. Safety always comes first. See the Snow Conditions & Safety section below.
How do I check my booking?
Tap "My bookings" at the top, enter the email you booked with, and we'll send you a login link to see your booking, payment status and payment button.
